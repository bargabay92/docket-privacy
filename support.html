Common questions

Where are my files stored?

On your iPhone, in Docket's own library. If you turn on iCloud sync, they're also kept in your private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. We don't have servers that store your documents.

I deleted a file by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted files stay in My Files → Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it and tap Restore.

How do I restore Docket Pro on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then go to Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Docket Pro screen.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → Docket, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. If you're in the free trial, cancelling before it ends means you pay nothing.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does Ask AI send my documents anywhere?

Ask AI answers from your document's own text. Writing Tools and automatic file names run on your iPhone. The optional cloud photo naming only runs after you agree to it, and sends a small, reduced image that isn't stored. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Search doesn't find words in my scan.

A photo or scan has no text until it's read. Open the file and tap Find text: Docket reads the page, and from then on the file can be found from Home too. For other languages, add them in Settings → Text Recognition first.

Docket doesn't appear in the Share Sheet.

In the Share Sheet, scroll the row of app icons to the end, tap More, and turn on Docket. For actions, scroll down and tap Edit Actions to add "Convert to PDF".

A web page didn't convert fully.

Try "Article only" for news and blog pages, or "One long page" to keep the page in one piece. Some sites block automated loading or need you to sign in; those may not convert.
